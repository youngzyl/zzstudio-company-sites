Before you write

Questions we get first.

If yours is not here, ask it directly — the answer will be more useful than a brochure.

Do you work with our existing models and infrastructure?

Yes. We are deliberately model-agnostic and infrastructure-agnostic. Hosted frontier models, open-weight models you self-host, or a mix — the vault, agent and skill layers are designed so the generation model is a swappable component.

Pipeline and index run in your own cloud account or on your own hardware. We do not host your retrieval logic.

Can it run entirely on-premise or air-gapped?

Yes, including the embedding and reranking models. It costs more engineering effort and typically trades some quality for locality, so we will quantify that trade-off during the diagnostic rather than after the contract.

How do you handle permissions and data residency?

Source-level access control is mapped during ingestion and enforced at retrieval, so the index itself never becomes a way around your existing rules. Data residency is a deployment choice: your region, your account, your keys.

What does a diagnostic actually produce?

A working retrieval or agent prototype over your own corpus, scored against a golden question set we build with your team, plus a written read on feasibility, cost, risk and a scoped build plan. It is yours to keep whether or not you continue with us.

Do you build the front-end as well?

We build the retrieval API, the agent runtime and the evaluation tooling, and we ship reference surfaces (a search UI, a chat surface, an operator console) so the system is usable from week one. For production front-ends inside a larger product we usually pair with your own team or a design partner.

How do you price engagements?

Fixed price per phase, agreed before it starts. The diagnostic is fixed-scope; build phases are quoted per milestone so you can stop between them. Advisory is a monthly retainer. No percentage-of-savings arrangements, no per-seat licensing on anything we write for you.

We are a small team. Is this only for enterprises?

No — the same three layers apply at any size. Small teams often get the most value from a lean vault plus skill management, because a handful of people cannot afford to re-derive the same answer every week.
