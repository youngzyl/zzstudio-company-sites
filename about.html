The studio

A small team that ships systems, not slide decks.

zzstudio is an applied AI studio in Taipei. We take on a handful of engagements at a time, stay close to the code, and measure ourselves by what still works a year after we leave.

Who we are

Engineers who got tired of pilots that never shipped.

We started zzstudio after years of watching good AI ideas die in the gap between a convincing demo and a system an organisation can actually depend on.

That gap is almost never about the model. It is about retrieval that ignores permissions, memory nobody designed, prompts nobody can version, evaluation that never happened, and a vendor who owns the critical path. Those are unglamorous engineering problems — and they are exactly the ones we like.

So we built a studio around three deliberately narrow offerings: a context vault that makes knowledge retrievable and accountable, a personal agent that can act on it safely, and a skill layer that keeps quality from drifting. Everything else we recommend, refer, or decline.

We work in English and 繁體中文, across APAC and US time zones, and we are just as comfortable in a regulated on-premise environment as in a greenfield cloud account.

Principles

Six rules we do not bend.

  1. Evidence before enthusiasm

    Nothing gets built until it has been tested against real questions on real data. A prototype that retrieves badly is worth more to you than a beautiful mock-up, because it tells you the truth early and cheaply.

  2. Measure the failure modes

    We report what the system gets wrong with the same care as what it gets right. Retrieval misses, leakage, refusal behaviour and cost outliers belong in the same document as the wins.

  3. Permissions are architecture

    Access control is designed into ingestion and retrieval from day one — never bolted on top of a search index as a filter someone remembers to apply.

  4. No proprietary critical path

    If we disappeared tomorrow, your system should keep running and your team should be able to change it. That rules out hosting your retrieval logic behind our API.

  5. Evaluation is the product

    The harness, the golden set and the review process are the durable assets. Models will be replaced several times over the life of the system; the tests are what make those swaps safe.

  6. Leave the team stronger

    We pair rather than deliver over the wall. Success is your engineer owning the architecture by the time we finish, not a dependency on our availability.

Taiwan

Why we build from Taipei.

Taiwan is a small island with an outsized concentration of people who make physical things work at scale. That culture — precision, tolerance for constraints, respect for the supply chain — turns out to be the right temperament for production AI.

Building here also keeps us close to the region's manufacturing, logistics and semiconductor teams, many of whom are now the ones being asked to put AI into operations with real sovereignty and residency requirements.
